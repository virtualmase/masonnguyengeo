# Build Record Integration Brief

## Recommendation

Create one new canonical site layer called **Applied Systems** with the internal label **Build Record**. It should be a compact, source-led index of selected systems Mason has built or is maintaining as prototypes. This gives the phrase “I am the niche” a public, inspectable structure: the research pages explain the method; the Build Record links to working artifacts.

The page should not be a conventional portfolio or a grid of marketing claims. Every record should answer four questions: **what is it, what is its current phase, what does the public link evidence, and what does it not claim?**

## Initial release: four records

| Priority | Record | Public purpose | Recommended site framing | Required boundary before linking |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cite Watch Pulse | A sweep-based console for tracking mentions and citations for `masonnguyengeo.com`. | **GEO observation console · active baseline** | It currently shows zero completed sweeps. | 4 | Atlas Nexus GEO | A systems-map prototype connecting GEO research clusters, experiments, and applied architecture. | **GEO systems-map prototype · research model** | Remove or separately evidence claims about citation counts, conversion figures, agent totals, lift, and real outcomes before the page is treated as a public proof source. |

## Supporting records: release later

Arctura Guard ICE, Arctura Core, and ChainScope are visually developed system studies. They fit a later **Experimental Systems** index, but not the first core authority set. Each public screen currently contains potentially factual institutional, performance, compliance, client, or partnership language that needs provenance before it is associated with Mason’s research-site authority.

Authentication-gated projects—Arctura Learn, ARM Ops, Core Forensic Pulse, CreatorBid Pulse, and NexusCore—should be assessed through a short public walkthrough before they are listed. Rewire Quantum Shift should remain offline from the main site until its broken route is repaired. Quantum Leap can belong in a broader experiments archive rather than the GEO/ARM authority path.

## Recommended page structure

The new `/applied-systems` page should use the same ivory/ink research system as the GEO Stack. It should begin with a short scope statement and a four-card record grid. Each card needs a **System**, **Phase**, **Public record**, **What it tests**, and **Boundary** field. Cards should link outward to the app and, where available, to an open source repository or a related Mason Nguyen GEO guide.

Example card framing:

> **Cite Watch Pulse**
>
> **Phase:** Active baseline
>
> **Tests:** A repeatable method for recording answer-engine mentions and citation observations.
>
> **Public record:** Application link; future dated observation log.
>
> **Boundary:** A dashboard is a measurement tool, not a performance result.

## Claim standard

> Describe the build, its phase, its public evidence, and its boundary. Do not borrow customer names, logos, user counts, compliance labels, market results, platform endorsement, or automated-agent outcomes as proof unless the relevant source is public and independently checkable.
