# GEO Stack Front-End Direction

## Reference patterns worth adapting

The AURE reference earns its professional feel through a strict editorial system rather than through decorative effects. Its strongest patterns are a framed hero with a narrow central narrative column, a small metadata rail, intentional headline contrast, numbered records that create progress through the page, a consistent field-note vocabulary, outlined modules with generous interior spacing, and a concrete illustrative record that interrupts the prose with a more tactile interface.

## Current GEO Stack diagnosis

The current page already has a compatible dark grid hero and an ivory research field, but the hero uses only the left half of its canvas and reads as a standard template. The six-layer grid appears early without a visual framework that explains the relationship between the layers. Long prose sections, standard cards, and a generic related-links rail create an even rhythm rather than a sequence of editorial moments.

## Refinement direction

Keep the existing ink, ivory, ultramarine, and periwinkle palette, along with IBM Plex Mono and Manrope. Adapt the reference through original components: a compact record rail, a hero field map, a “stack specimen” diagram that makes the six layers visible as an ordered system, alternating full-bleed dark records and ivory reading blocks, improved section labels, restrained dividers, a real-source protocol card, and a more deliberate related-research panel.

The page should feel like a maintained research artifact—calm, spacious, and inspectable—not a product landing page or a copied AURE design.
