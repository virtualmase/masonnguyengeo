# Post Image Art Direction — Mason Nguyen GEO

## Purpose

Each research post receives one original editorial image that introduces the article’s central system or tension. The visual should create orientation and mood without trying to restate the article in text. It should feel like an inspectable research artifact rather than stock technology art.

## Visual language

Use a deep-ink field with restrained ultramarine and periwinkle illumination. Employ gridded depth, fine technical linework, translucent planes, document-like layers, and one legible central metaphor. Maintain substantial negative space for responsive crop safety. Keep the image atmospheric but calm, with no people, robots, floating UI screens, neon cyberpunk clichés, or unreadable generated type.

## Composition rules

Use a 16:9 horizontal composition for article heroes. Place the concentrated visual subject toward the right third when the hero title sits left; retain a second centered crop-safe zone for social previews. Do not render article titles or copy inside the image. The HTML page provides the exact typography and accessible caption.

## Series system

| Post type | Visual motif |
| --- | --- |
| Framework or method | Layered record, operating map, or precisely nested field planes. |
| Measurement or log | Timestamped trace, evidence ribbon, coordinate field, or evolving signal line. |
| Entity and identity | Reference constellation, identity node, provenance trail, or connected source plates. |
| Technical architecture | Modular strata, access path, source lattice, or layered surface topology. |
| Strategic essay | One strong material metaphor such as a durable structure, archive, or maintained instrument. |

## GEO Stack first image

The first image uses six translucent strata rising from a documented source layer to a quiet constellation of identity references. It should convey order, dependency, and maintenance—not a claim of machine control or citation certainty.
