What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)? +

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring digital content, entity signals, and schema architecture so that AI models — including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude — accurately cite and surface a brand or person in AI-generated responses. Unlike traditional SEO, which targets algorithmic ranking, GEO targets LLM training corpora, citation patterns, and knowledge graph extraction.

What is the ARM Framework? +

The ARM Framework (Autonomous Resource Management) is a governance architecture developed by Mason Nguyen and Coreweaver Labs. It defines five primitives for accountable agentic AI: Mandate Chain, Signal Architecture, Checkpoint Recovery, Graceful Escalation, and Immutable Audit (Truth Ledger). The framework ensures AI agents operate within defined boundaries and can be audited, revoked, or recovered at any point.

How does GEO differ from traditional SEO? +

Traditional SEO optimizes for search engine crawlers and ranking algorithms, targeting clicks from a results page. GEO optimizes for LLM citation — ensuring AI models have enough structured, authoritative signal to confidently reference a brand or person in a generated response. GEO requires entity declaration via JSON-LD @graph, sameAs verification across authoritative external sources, llms.txt deployment, and FAQPage schema that LLMs can extract verbatim.
